# JAWZR product gallery: designer brief

**Source:** Jawliner's product-page gallery slides 2–12 (folder `Products/Gallery/examples`).
**Scope:** every element *except the photo itself*. That covers headlines, callouts, captions, tables, citations, badges and layout. Each slide has a one-line **image note** so the text fits the picture, but the picture is your call.

---

## 0. Global style (applies to all slides)

| | Jawliner (reference) | JAWZR (use this) |
|---|---|---|
| Format | 700 × 700 px square | **1600 × 1600 px** square, exported as WebP/JPG (shown at ~600 px on the site) |
| Headline | Lime, heavy rounded sans, uppercase, top-left, ends with a full stop | **Big Shoulders Display 900**, uppercase, top-left, ends with a full stop. White on photos and dark backgrounds; ink `#121614` on light backgrounds |
| Accent colour | Lime `#D7F205`-ish | **Cobalt `#2440E6`**; lighter `#5C78FF` for dark backgrounds. **Don't use lime: that's Jawliner's look** |
| Callouts | White uppercase label + thin lime line + ⊕ dot on the subject | Same system: white (or ink) uppercase label in **JetBrains Mono 600** + 1.5 px cobalt line + cobalt dot with a white ⊕ |
| Body / captions | White sentence case | **IBM Plex Sans** 400/600, sentence case |
| Dark slides | Navy gradient with a light "floor" glow | Navy-to-ocean gradient like the bag (`#0C2A4F` → `#2F78C4`) with a soft light floor |
| Gradient overlay on photos | Lime fade from the bottom | Cobalt/navy fade from the bottom, 0 → 70% |
| Citations | "STUDIES:" label, author + year bold, title in caps | Same layout: "STUDY:" / "STUDIES:" label in accent, author + year bold, rest regular, 8–9 pt equivalent |
| Logo | Not on most slides | Small JAWZR wordmark (`brand/jawzr-wordmark.svg`) bottom-right on slides without a pack, optional |

**Rules for every slide:**
- No visible-result promises: no "sharper jawline", "jaw definition", "results in X weeks".
- No endorsements, press logos or "as seen on" we don't have.
- No before/after photos that aren't real, consenting JAWZR customers.
- No medical claims: no TMJ, posture, breathing, "fixes" anything.
- Spelling: British, matching the site (**flavour**, not flavor).

## Slide 4 (ref `5bb.webp`): the science: muscle, not bone

**Purpose:** explain *what* is trained, and stay honest that bone doesn't change.
**Image note:** side profile, with a graphic overlay of the skull/jaw and the masseter highlighted in **cobalt glow** (theirs is lime).

| Element | Position | Jawliner text | **JAWZR text** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline (3 lines) | Top-left | THE SCIENCE — IT'S MUSCLE, NOT BONE. | **THE SCIENCE: IT'S MUSCLE, NOT BONE.** |
| Callout 1 | Left, dot on the masseter | MASSETER MUSCLE GROWS WITH LOAD | **MASSETER: TRAINED BY RESISTANCE** |
| Callout 2 | Right, dot on the jaw bone | BONE STRUCTURE STAYS THE SAME | **BONE STRUCTURE STAYS THE SAME** |
| Citations label | Bottom-left | STUDIES: | **STUDIES:** |
| Citation 1 | Below the label | RAADSHEER M.C. ET AL. (1999): Contribution of jaw muscle size and craniofacial morphology to human bite force magnitude. Journal of Dental Research, 78(1), 31–42. | **Keep this one** (it backs the muscle → bite force link) |
| Citation 2 | Below citation 1 | LIEBERMAN D.E. ET AL. (2004): Effects of food processing on masticatory strain and craniofacial growth in a retrognathic face. Journal of Human Evolution, 46(6), 655–677. | **Replace with:** **JUNG H.-J. ET AL. (2024):** Effects of gum chewing training on occlusal force, masseter muscle thickness and mandibular shape: a randomised controlled clinical trial. Journal of Oral Rehabilitation. doi:10.1111/joor.13830 |

**Why the swap:** Lieberman 2004 is an animal study on growth in young animals, so it doesn't support adult training. Jung 2024 is a human trial: chewing training raised bite force, with no change in jaw shape. That matches the "not bone" headline. Don't add "grows" to the masseter callout, because this trial didn't find a thickness change.
