# Compliance checklist (EU / Lithuania)

Not legal advice; confirm with a Lithuanian food/consumer lawyer before launch.

## Food law
- [ ] Register MB Monokodas as a food business operator with VMVT, even for distribution only.
- [ ] Label in Lithuanian (and each target-market language): name, ingredients with additive categories, allergens, net weight, best before, storage, operator name/address, nutrition per 100 g.
- [ ] "With sweeteners" (Reg. 1169/2011 Annex III).
- [ ] "Excessive consumption may produce laxative effects" — mandatory when added polyols > 10% (Reg. 1169/2011 Annex III; see Reg. 2021/1175). Both Hard and Ultra Hard almost certainly qualify.
- [ ] Caffeine variant (if ever added): "Contains caffeine. Not recommended for children or pregnant or breast-feeding women" + mg per portion.
- [ ] Same mandatory info on the product page before purchase (Reg. 1169/2011 Art. 14).

## Claims (Reg. 1924/2006)
- [ ] No health claims beyond the authorised EU register (sugar-free gum dental claims only if conditions of use are met).
- [ ] No disease/medical claims (TMJ, breathing/oxygen, asymmetry).
- [ ] "Sugar-free" only if ≤0.5 g sugars/100 g.
- [ ] Hardness multipliers only with documented test data.
- [ ] No stock photos presented as results; before/after only with real, consenting, documented customers.

## Consumer law / e-commerce
- [ ] Company details on site: name, code, VAT, address, email.
- [ ] Unit price per kg next to price.
- [ ] Discounts: "was" price = lowest price in previous 30 days (Omnibus). No permanent fake "47% off".
- [ ] 14-day withdrawal right + model form; state hygiene exception for opened gum.
- [ ] Reviews: explain verification; no fake or bought reviews.
- [ ] Subscriptions: clear price, frequency, easy cancel, pre-renewal reminder.
- [ ] Terms, privacy policy (GDPR), cookie consent.
- [ ] OSS VAT registration for EU B2C sales above €10k/yr.

## Age and advertising
- [ ] Minimum age on pack and site (Jawliner 14+; consider 16+).
- [ ] No ad targeting of under-18s; avoid personal-attribute and before/after formats Meta flags.
- [ ] Run ad copy through a claim check before launch.

## Brand / IP
- [ ] Trademark search (EUIPO, LT VPB). "Cutline" not checked.
- [ ] Don't use "JAWLINER" or similar names, their photos or copy.
- [ ] If factory packaging resembles Jawliner's, change the trade dress.
