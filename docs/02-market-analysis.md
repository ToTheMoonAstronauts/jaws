# Market analysis: jaw training gum

Researched 2026-10-31. Market-size reports vary a lot; treat as directional.

## Size

- Jaw exercisers market: USD 98.7M (2025), forecast USD 225.4M by 2034, 9.4% CAGR (Intel Market Research).
- Broader "jaw exercise device" reports: ~USD 0.4B (2025), 7.2% CAGR (Market Intelo).
- Amazon US "jaw exerciser": top 10 average price $14.97, avg rating 4.3; top 3 listings take ~49% of clicks. Cheap silicone trainers dominate volume (ASINsight).

Positioning gaps:
1. Trust: category under heavy press criticism; nobody owns "honest / evidence-led".
2. Flavour that lasts on a hard piece (top complaint).
3. Baltic/Nordic/CEE local shipping (Jawliner ships from DE).
4. A real training programme (timer/app/progress), not just gum.

## Demand drivers

- "Looksmaxxing": Gen Z / Gen Alpha male appearance-optimisation trend on TikTok; "chewing-maxxing" is a named sub-practice. Growing "looksmaxxing economy" (YPulse/BoF, Apr 2026).
- Mewing, jaw trainers and hard gum promoted together.
- Strong backlash: Johns Hopkins (Aug 2026) warning about risks to boys and teens. Expect platform/regulator scrutiny of ads reaching minors.

## Scientific evidence

- Jung et al. 2024 (Yonsei RCT, n=58): gum chewing 3x/day for 6 months raised max bite force (significant at 3 months) but NO change in masseter thickness or jaw shape. Ordinary gum.
- Park et al. 2020 (older adults, 6 weeks, chewing device 20 min/day): bite force and masseter thickness increased.
- Preschool study: hard gum 2x5 min/day for 4 weeks raised bite force.
- Long-duration gum chewing linked to TMD symptoms (clicking, pain).
- Dentists (ADA, Hackensack, NYT, CBS): little evidence for a sharper jawline; risks are TMJ strain, broken teeth, loose fillings, headaches. Bigger masseter = wider/squarer face, not sharper. Body fat loss is the main driver of jawline visibility.

Implication: brand around bite strength + training routine + honesty about body fat. Avoid "sculpt your jawline in 2 weeks".

## Target customer

- Men 18–30, gym-going, appearance-focused, TikTok/Instagram/YouTube Shorts.
- Secondary: older lifters, daily gum chewers wanting a "functional" gum.
- Do not target under-18s.
