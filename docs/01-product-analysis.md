## Formulation

**Hard**: gum base, sorbitol, maltitol, flavourings, malic acid, citric acid, gum arabic, calcium carbonate, steviol glycosides, sucralose, carnauba wax. Vegan.
Per 100 g: 588 kJ / 140 kcal, carbohydrates 59 g, sugars 0 g.

**Ultra Hard**: gum base, maltitol, flavouring, gum arabic, carnauba wax, talc. Vegan.
Per 100 g: 202 kJ / 48 kcal, carbohydrates 20 g, sugars 0 g. Marketed as "0% synthetic sweeteners".

Notes:
- Flavour sits mainly in the outer coating to keep the core hard; flavour fades within seconds to minutes (Ultra). Most common review complaint.
- Ultra Hard is reusable "for days": rinse, dry, store in the box, warm up before reuse.
- They say manufacturing needs manual steps because normal machines can't handle the hardness (price justification).

## Usage protocol (theirs)

1. Warm up on the molars, increase pressure gradually (cold piece can crack).
2. 3 min slow chewing with increasing pressure.
3. 2 min faster, controlled.
4. 3 min rest.
5. Repeat the block 2–3 times.
6. Store and reuse. Train every other day.

Inconsistency: pages say both "10–15 min every other day" and "15–20 min". Our draft uses 10–15 min.

Their safety copy: not under 14; stop if pain; consult doctor/orthodontist with existing jaw problems; "you accept full responsibility". Ultra Hard: not with loose fillings, implants, veneers.

## Review insights

Positives: very hard, "burn" after 5 min, reusable, good flavours on Hard (blueberry, mango).
Negatives: flavour fades fast on Ultra, sugar glaze annoying on first bite, small pack for the price, expensive shipping outside EU, occasional crumbly batch, some say Ultra isn't harder than Hard, one report of ear/jaw noises after over-chewing.
Users repeatedly note results depend on body fat.
