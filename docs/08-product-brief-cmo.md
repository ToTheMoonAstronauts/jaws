# JAWZR: product brief for the CMO

*Status 3 Nov 2026 · pre-launch, fake door test · English site live, Lithuanian site paused*

## TL;DR

- **What:** hard chewing gum for jaw-muscle training, in two levels (Hard, Ultra Hard). Made in the same factory as Jawliner, the EU category leader.
- **Brand:** JAWZR. The mascot is a shark. Tagline: *"Train your jaw. Unleash the shark."* Domain: jawzr.com (DNS not connected yet).
- **Offer:** a 30-day daily plan. 1 pack (40 pieces) covers it. Prices are €19,95 for 1 pack, €34,95 for 3 and €59,95 for 6. That's cheaper than Jawliner (€24,95 for 1, €39,95 for 3).
- **Stage:** a fake door test. The full shop and checkout work, but **no payment is taken**. On "Pay", the visitor sees "you're early, you have not been charged" and gets an EARLY20 launch code. We capture email, cart and payment-method choice.
- **What we need from marketing:** paid traffic to the live site to measure real purchase intent, i.e. add-to-cart, checkout start and "pay" clicks per € spent.

**Live site:** https://tothemoonastronauts.github.io/jaws/ (product page: `/product.html`, checkout: `/checkout.html`)

Both are **sugar-free and vegan**. Both need the warning **"Contains sweeteners. Excessive consumption may produce laxative effects."**

### How to use it: the 30-day plan

This is the official instruction everywhere on the site. Use it word for word in ads and creatives.

- Chew **every day for 30 days**, with **a fresh piece each day**.
- **Full day:** 3 rounds of (3 min slow and heavy → 2 min faster → 3 min rest), 10–15 min in total.
- **Light day** (the next day): 1 round, about 8 min.
- **Week 1:** Hard every day. **From week 2:** Ultra Hard on full days, Hard on light days.
- **Never more than 15 minutes a day.** Stop if there's sharp pain, clicking or locking.

Why it's built this way, commercially:

- Daily use plus a fresh piece each day means about 30 pieces a month.
- The plan needs both levels from week 2, which pushes the bundle.
- We **removed every "reusable piece" claim**. Jawliner tells people to rinse and reuse; we don't.

Jawliner says every other day. We chose daily with alternating light days to get a daily habit while keeping recovery days.

## 4. Audience

- **Primary:** men aged 18–30 who go to the gym and care about appearance. They're on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, in the looksmaxxing and mewing space.
- **Secondary:** older lifters, and people who already chew gum daily and want a "functional" one.
- **Never target under-18s.** Johns Hopkins (Aug 2026) and dentists are publicly criticising hard gum aimed at teens. The site says 14+; ad targeting must be 18+.

## 5. Positioning and messaging

Gaps in the market that we can own:

1. **Honest and evidence-led:** the category is under press fire, and nobody owns trust yet.
2. **A real routine, not just gum:** the 30-day plan, a session timer on the product page, and full/light days.
3. **Local and fast:** we ship from Lithuania, while competitors ship from Germany, the UK or the US.
4. **Price:** under the leader at every pack size.

**Messaging pillars** (safe to use):

- *Hard gum for a stronger jaw* (headline A, live)
- *Train it like a set at the gym:* rounds, rest, progression
- *Every day for 30 days, 8–15 minutes a day*
- *Built far harder than regular gum:* every chew is a rep
- *Train the masseter:* Plan B headline, on the v1 site at `/v1.html`
- *Sugar-free, vegan, 40 pieces, ships from the EU*

## 6. Claims: do and don't (read before writing ads)

| ✅ OK | ❌ Not OK | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Train your jaw muscles" / "stronger bite" | "Sculpt your jawline in 2 weeks", before/after photos | No evidence for visible change; Meta personal-attribute and before/after rules |
| "You'll feel it from the first session" | "See the difference in 30 days" | We chose **no result promise** |
| "Pairs best with lower body fat" | "Fixes asymmetry", "prevents TMJ", "better breathing/oxygen" | Medical/disease claims are banned for food (Reg. 1924/2006) |
| "Much harder than regular gum" | "15x harder" | Only allowed with our own test data |
| "Jaw training gum" | **"Focus boost"** (it's on the bag mock-up) | Unauthorised cognitive health claim; **remove from the bag** |
| Real, verified reviews (after launch) | Fake reviews, invented testimonials, "250,000 customers" | Banned (EU Omnibus, FTC); the site shows clearly labelled sample cards only |
| Real "was" prices | Permanent fake "47% off" | Omnibus: "was" price = lowest price in the last 30 days |
| Our own creators | "Endorsed by Dr. Mike Mew" / Jawliner content | That's their endorsement and their IP |

**Safety line for creatives:** "Not for under 14s. No more than 15 min a day. Stop if it hurts."

## 7. Fake door test: what we measure

**Visitor flow:** landing → product page (pick hardness, flavour, pack) → cart → checkout (email, name, country, payment method) → **"Pay"** → a screen saying they haven't been charged, with the EARLY20 code.

**Data** goes to Supabase (EU) in three tables: `events`, `leads` and `withdrawals`. There's also a daily `funnel_daily` view.

| Event | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `page_view`, `product_view` | Reach and interest |
| `add_to_cart` | Intent: level, flavour, pack |
| `checkout_start` | Strong intent |
| `payment_method` | Which payment option people pick |
| `pay_click` + a `leads` row | **Main KPI:** would have bought (email, cart, total, payment method) |
| `newsletter_signup` | Soft lead |

What to know about the data:

- **UTM tags are captured on every event and lead.** Tag every ad link with `utm_source`, `utm_campaign` and `utm_content` so we can read cost per `pay_click` per creative.
- **Analytics events only fire for visitors who accept cookies** (GDPR). Leads from checkout are always captured. Expect events to undercount, so use leads as the source of truth.
- **Suggested read-outs:**
  - cost per checkout start
  - cost per pay_click
  - add-to-cart rate
  - pack mix: the share choosing 3 or 6 packs, which is a signal for average order value
  - Hard vs Ultra split
  - flavour ranking
  - payment-method split

## 8. Market snapshot

- **Jaw exerciser market:** about USD 99M (2025), growing ~9% a year. This is directional only.
- **Competitors:**
  - Jawliner (DE, the leader)
  - Rockjaw (UK, mastic, caffeine variants)
  - Stronger Gum (FI)
  - Jawz, Jawcko, GymGum (US)
  - Falim (TR, cheap and softer)
- **Top complaints about Jawliner** (our opportunities):
  - Ultra's flavour fades in seconds
  - Small pack for the price
  - Expensive shipping outside Germany
  - The occasional crumbly batch
- **Evidence:**
  - Chewing training raises bite force (a Yonsei RCT, at 3 and 6 months).
  - There's no proven change in jaw shape.
  - Body fat is the main driver of how visible the jawline is.
  - Overuse is linked to jaw-joint (TMD) symptoms.
