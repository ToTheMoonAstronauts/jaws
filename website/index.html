Masseter

Same jaw, stronger muscle

The muscle that shapes your jawline

The masseter sits right at the angle of your jaw. It's the muscle you feel bulge when you clench. Soft food and regular gum barely load it. Hard gum makes it work against real resistance, the same way weights train any other muscle.

  • Targets the masseter, your main chewing muscle
  • Full and light days, like a gym plan
  • Shows best when paired with lower body fat

Training advice

Chew it the right way

Soft, processed food leaves your jaw with almost no work. Hard gum gives it real resistance, but technique decides whether you train the right muscle.

  • Mouth closed, back teeth, not your cheeks
  • 10–15 minutes a day for 30 days, never more than 15
  • Sore for more than a day? Take a break
  • Pakartok tokią sesiją 2–3 kartus.

How it works

Every day, for 30 days

~1 min

Warm it up

Place the gum on your back molars and bite down gradually until it softens slightly.

Full day · 10–15 min

Chew in rounds

3 minutes slow and heavy, 2 minutes faster, 3 minutes rest. Repeat two or three times.

Next day · ~8 min

Light day

One round with Hard. The muscle recovers while you keep the habit. Then a full day again.

The 30-day plan. Chew every day for 30 days, with a fresh piece each day. Full day: 3 rounds, 10–15 minutes. Light day (the next day): 1 round, about 8 minutes. Week 1: Hard every day. From week 2: Ultra Hard on full days, Hard on light days. Never more than 15 minutes a day.

FAQ

Questions

How long until I see results?

You'll feel your jaw muscles working from the first session. Visible change takes consistent training over weeks to months, and it shows most when your body fat is lower.

How often should I chew it?

Every day for 30 days, alternating full and light days: a full session (3 rounds, 10–15 minutes) one day, one round (about 8 minutes) the next. Never more than 15 minutes a day. The light days let the muscle recover without breaking the habit.

Hard or Ultra Hard: which should I start with?

Start with Hard in week 1: it has more flavour and lets you learn the technique. From week 2, the plan uses Ultra Hard on full days and Hard on light days, so you'll want both.

Why does it cost more than shop gum?

It's a training tool, not candy. The dense gum base takes far more raw material, and the hardness needs slower production. Each piece is one full session, so a workout costs €0,22–0,60 depending on the hardness and bundle size.

Is it safe for my teeth and jaw?

Chew on your back molars, stop if you feel sharp pain, and don't use it with jaw-joint problems, loose fillings, implants or braces. Not for under-14s.
