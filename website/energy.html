    How it works
    1. Warm it up. Place the gum on your back molars and bite down gradually. A cold, hard bite can crack it.
    2. 3 min: slow, heavy chews with pressure building on every bite.
    3. 2 min: faster chews, still controlled. Switch sides evenly.
    4. 3 min: rest with your jaw relaxed.
    5. Repeat the block two or three times, 10–15 minutes in total.
    6. Bin it. Throw the piece away after the session. Start every session with a fresh one.
    7. Tomorrow: light day. One round, about 8 minutes, ideally with Hard. Alternate full and light days, every day for 30 days.

    Safety information

    Full details: Safety Instructions.

    Contains caffeine: 40 mg per piece. Not recommended for children, pregnant or breastfeeding women, or people sensitive to caffeine. No more than 4 pieces a day, and count coffee, tea and energy drinks too. Avoid it in the evening if caffeine affects your sleep.

    This is a training product, much harder than regular gum. No more than 15 minutes a day; alternate full and light days.

    Not for under 14s. Never swallow the piece. Keep away from small children.

    Stop if you feel pain. A muscle burn that fades within a day is normal; sharp pain, clicking or locking is not.

    Ask a dentist first if you have jaw-joint problems, loose fillings, crowns, veneers, implants or braces.

    FAQ

    Caffeine gum questions

    How much caffeine is in one piece?

    40 mg, about half a single espresso. A 40-piece pack holds 1.6 g in total.

    How many pieces can I chew a day?

    No more than 4. EFSA considers up to 400 mg of caffeine a day safe for healthy adults, so count your coffee, tea and energy drinks too. For jaw training you only need one piece per session.

    Who shouldn't use it?

    Children, pregnant or breastfeeding women and people sensitive to caffeine. It's also a hard training gum, so the usual rules apply: not for under 14s, and ask a dentist first if you have jaw-joint problems or dental work.

    Is it as hard as JAWZR Hard?

    Yes. It's the same hardness as Hard, in mint, with caffeine added. If you want more resistance, train with Ultra Hard on full days.
