Does chewing gum burn calories?

JAWZR team · 4 November 2026 · 3 min read

You'll see claims that chewing gum 'boosts your metabolism'. It does raise energy use a little, but it isn't a weight-loss tool.

In shortA 1999 study in the New England Journal of Medicine measured about 11 kcal an hour extra while chewing gum, roughly 20% above resting. That's real but small, and a 15-minute training session burns only a few calories.

What was measured

Researchers at the Mayo Clinic measured the energy use of seven volunteers at rest, and again while they chewed calorie-free gum at a steady 100 chews a minute. Energy use rose from about 58 to about 70 kcal an hour, around 11 kcal an hour more, or roughly 20% above resting.1

The honest maths

  • A 15-minute JAWZR session at that rate is about 3 kcal.
  • That's less than one bite of an apple.
  • A piece of gum has a few calories of its own. Check the nutrition table on the pack.

The researchers did calculate that chewing all day, every day could add up over a year. Nobody should train their jaw all day, though. The JAWZR plan caps sessions at 15 minutes for a reason.

So why train your jaw?

For the muscles, not for the calories. Hard gum is resistance training for your masseter: stronger bite, better endurance and a habit that takes 15 minutes. If your goal is a sharper jawline, losing body fat matters as much as the muscle. That comes from diet and overall activity, not from gum.
