3. Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising cookies. We store in your browser: your cart (needed for the shop to work, no consent required), your cookie choice, and, only if you accept analytics, a random ID that links your visits. You can change your choice any time under Cookie settings. If we later add advertising pixels (for example Meta or Google), we will ask for separate consent first and update this policy.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA): database for orders, pre-orders and analytics. Data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA): website hosting.
  • Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd.): delivers the fonts on our site; your browser connects to Google's servers.

Where a provider is based outside the EU, transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (GDPR Art. 45–46).

5. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Email [contact email]; we reply within one month. You can also complain to the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija), L. Sapiegos g. 17, 10312 Vilnius, vdai.lrv.lt, or to the authority in your own country.

6. Children

Our shop is not aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

7. Changes

We'll update this page when our processing changes and show the date at the top.
